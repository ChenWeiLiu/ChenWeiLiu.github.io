Salt water 鹽水

Salt water has a lot of salt. Ocean water is salt water. We cannot drink it.

Fresh water 淡水

Fresh water has little or no salt. We use it for drinking, washing, cooking, and farming.

Surface water 地表水

Water on or near the surface of Earth.

lakes 湖泊 ponds 池塘 rivers 河流 streams 溪流 rain 雨水 reservoirs 水庫

Groundwater 地下水

Rainwater soaks into the ground and fills the spaces between rocks and soil.

雨水滲進地下，填滿岩石和土壤的縫隙。井水是常見的地下水。抽水會改變 water table，地下水位。

Most drinking water comes from groundwater in wells, lakes, rivers, and streams.

Clean water and a filter

Earth does not run out of water. The water cycle keeps the total the same. Clean drinking water can get scarce because only about 1% is usable, pollution makes clean water less, and more people need it.

棉花、沙或小石子可以做成過濾器，把髒東西擋住。

Conserve 節約

Conserve means to use something carefully and not waste it.

short showers turn off the tap half flush collect rain fix drips reuse water
